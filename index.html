§ 06

Questions, answered

The things people ask before their first order.

How do I pay?

You don't pay on this site — we never ask for card details here. Place your order, we reply within a day to confirm stock and delivery, and collect payment by UPI or bank transfer at that point. Card payments are coming once our payment provider finishes verification.

What does "clean label" actually mean?

Every ingredient on the pack is a real food you can recognise. No artificial preservatives, no hidden fillers, no proprietary blends that hide how little of something is in there. Tap any product to see its full ingredient list before you buy.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within 1–2 business days and delivery usually takes 3–6 business days anywhere in India. Shipping is free with no minimum order. You'll get tracking details on the phone number and email you give at checkout.

I have a food allergy — is this safe for me?

Every product page lists its allergens. Most of our range contains milk, and several contain tree nuts or peanuts. Everything is packed in a facility that also handles milk and nuts, so we can't guarantee against trace cross-contact. Always check the physical pack before eating.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you in full, at no cost to you. Because these are food products we can't accept returns on opened packs otherwise.

Can I change or cancel after ordering?

Yes — email us your order ID as soon as you can. We can change or cancel anything right up until it ships.
